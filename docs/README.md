### Abstract

Real estate investment has long been considered a valuable asset due to its
potential to generate a steady stream of income. The emergence of blockchain
technology and smart contracts has led to the tokenization of assets, which can
increase liquidity, facilitate access for small investors, and enhance
transparency. However, previous research, despite its limited number, has
primarily focused on a single aspect of blockchain applications in real estate,
often neglecting challenges such as co-ownership asset management and the
collection and management of rental and service fees for multi-ownership units.
This research addresses these limitations by presenting a comprehensive approach
to developing and implementing a real estate investment management system. The
main innovation of the research is the combined use of different token standards
to represent and manage assets. The system includes features such as full and
fractional tokenization of assets and provision of individual and shared
ownership, management of shared units through a decentralized autonomous
organization (DAO), comprehensive rental and service fees management, and
transparent building manager elections on the blockchain. The findings indicate
that this system can effectively increase liquidity and access to the real estate
market and reduce transaction costs by reducing the need for intermediaries.
Comprehensive testing and associated evaluations ensure the correctness of the
proposed system's performance and cost-effectiveness. Additionally, the use of
blockchain enhances transparency and trust in the market and provides efficient
solutions to existing problems. This research demonstrates that real estate
tokenization with blockchain can transform the traditional real estate market.

**Keywords**: Blockchain, Tokenization, Smart Contract, NFT, Ethereum, Real Estate

املاک را متحول کند.

### Chapters

1. Introduction — problem statement, objectives, research questions and hypotheses
2. Literature review — blockchain, Ethereum, smart contracts, token standards,
   asset tokenization and prior work on blockchain in real estate
3. Proposed method — system architecture, tokenization model and contract design
4. Implementation and evaluation — tests, gas measurements and cost analysis
5. Discussion and conclusion

## Proposal Information

The research proposal (فرم پیشنهاد تحقیق) was defended in **Summer 1402
(Summer 2023)** under the same title, with Davood Bahrepour, Ph.D. as supervisor
and Seyed Reza Kamel Tabbakh, Ph.D. as advisor. It sets out the problem
statement, research questions and hypotheses, the literature review, the proposed
two-phase tokenization method (ERC-721 whole units, then ERC-1155 fractions) and
the planned evaluation — the thesis then reports the implemented result.
